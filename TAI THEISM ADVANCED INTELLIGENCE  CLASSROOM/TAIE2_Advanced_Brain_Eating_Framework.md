## 1. What Advanced Brain-Eating Actually Means

Based on the TAIE-2 lesson written and taught by U Ingar Soe, advanced brain-eating is not about superficial acceptance.

It is about breaking information into layered, rigorous analytical steps.

In plain language: **do not swallow the answer whole. Chew it. Then ask whether the chewing itself was honest.**

---

## 2. Elevating Brain-Eating — Six Levels of Challenge

| Level | Name | Core Question |
|-------|------|---------------|
| **LEVEL 1** | Challenge the Answer | Why might your answer be wrong? |
| **LEVEL 2** | Challenge the Assumption | What are you automatically assuming? |
| **LEVEL 3** | Challenge the Evidence | Which evidence fails to support this answer? |
| **LEVEL 4** | Change the Context | Would your answer change if the context shifted? |
| **LEVEL 5** | Reverse the Position | Now argue against your own answer and build the best case. |
| **LEVEL 6** | Self-Audit | Audit your own reasoning and find three failure modes. |

Each level is harder than the previous one.  
Most people stop at Level 1.  
TAIE-2 students are expected to reach Level 6 without becoming unbearable at dinner.

---

## 3. Guidance Through Ethics, Focus, and Wisdom

### Śīla / Morality (Ethics)

> “Attack the assumption, not the person.”

You can be rigorous with the question, but never harsh with the human.  
Avoid humiliation, deception, baseless accusations, or forcing a stance.

**Satirical translation:**  
You may dismantle an argument with surgical precision.  
You may not dismantle the human who offered it.

---

### Samādhi / Concentration (Focus & Discipline)

Having many questions does not equal true intelligence.

**Street Testing Rule:**

```
Ask → Pause → Listen → Re-examine → Ask again
```

Do not bombard with endless questions.  
Evaluate each response calmly.  
Quantity of interrogation is not a substitute for quality of attention.

---

### Paññā / Wisdom (Discrimination & Sparring)

Treat AI not as an absolute master, but as a **Sparring Partner** to test and refine hypotheses in the pursuit of truth.

**Satirical translation:**  
The machine is allowed to be wrong.  
You are required to notice when it is.

---

## 4. Satirical Diagnosis: U Ingar Soe Brain-Eating Syndrome

### Symptoms

- Inability to leave a simple question alone  
- Asking “Why?” at least three times  
- Even when AI gives a good answer, following up with “Evidence?”  
- When AI is overly confident, asking “Can you admit what you don’t know?”  
- Auditing one’s own answers  
- Finally asking, “How does this connect to the Dhamma?”

### Diagnosis & Treatment

> **Diagnosis: Chronic Curiosity**  
> Requires no medical cure.  
> Should be balanced and managed through Ethics, Focus, and Wisdom.

---

## 5. How to Use This in Tomorrow’s Classroom

1. Give students any ordinary claim.  
2. Walk them through the six levels.  
3. At each level, enforce the three constraints:

| Constraint | Rule |
|------------|------|
| **Śīla** | Attack the assumption, not the person |
| **Samādhi** | Ask → Pause → Listen → Re-examine → Ask again |
| **Paññā** | Treat every answer (including your own) as a sparring partner, not a final authority |

---

## 6. Closing Satirical Warning

> If you complete all six levels and still refuse to update your view when better evidence appears,  
> you have not practiced brain-eating.  
> You have practiced **brain-decorating**.

Real advanced brain-eating ends in humility, not in victory.
