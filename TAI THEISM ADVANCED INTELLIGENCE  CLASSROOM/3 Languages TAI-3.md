## I. Introduction — บทนำ — နိဒါန်း

### 🇬🇧 English

TAI-3 Classroom 53 is an intelligence-analysis lesson designed to train students to distinguish between **what is possible**, **what is actually happening**, **why it is happening**, and **what may happen next**, rather than jumping from a single report or piece of evidence to an unverified conclusion. Using conscription pressure, passport difficulties, and economic pressures as examples of potential Push Factors, the lesson asks what these indicators can legitimately demonstrate and what remains unknown. Its central calibration rules are **Claim Size ≤ Evidence Capacity** and **Potentiality ≠ Prediction ≠ Proven Reality**, with **Reality Before Narrative** serving as the core discipline.

## III. Push Factors

| Factor | Description | Warning |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| **1. Conscription pressure** | Forced service risk, arrest risk, narrowed future options | Presence of pressure ≠ everyone will leave |
| **2. Economic / career dead-end** | Job scarcity, education disruption, income collapse | Do not assume purely political motive |
| **3. Passport / administrative trap** | Renewal blocks, administrative barriers | Administrative Barrier ≠ proven Political Intention |

---

## IV. Three Masters Matrix

| Master | Method | Key Question |
|--------|--------|--------------|
| **Holmes** | Elimination & sequential testing | Why are we adding what the evidence does not say? H1 (can move) does not prove H5 (will join armed groups). |
| **Columbo** | Persistent metric inquiry | How many? How many actually moved? What % joined armed groups? Who measured this? |
| **Hoover** | Institutional-scale caution | Individual recruitment event ≠ regional or national trend. Scale carefully. |

### Holmes Sequential Claims

| Hypothesis | Content | Requirement |
|------------|---------|-------------|
| **H1** | People *can* move | Possibility |
| **H2** | People *are* moving | Measurement |
| **H3** | Where are they moving? | Destination data |
| **H4** | Why are they moving? | Causation evidence |
| **H5** | Will they join armed groups? | Higher-order claim |

> **Holmes Rule:** Proving H1 does not automatically prove H5.

## V. TAI-3 Calibration Engine

### STEP 1 — CLAIM

> “Due to conscription and passport pressure, large numbers of Gen Z will move to liberated areas and join resistance.”

### STEP 2 — EVIDENCE

- Recruitment reports  
- Passport difficulty reports  
- Fear-of-conscription testimonies  

These support possible Push Pressure, but do **not** yet measure national-scale movement, destinations, or armed participation rates.

### STEP 3 — ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

| Code | Alternative |
|------|-------------|
| **A** | Economic survival / job seeking |
| **B** | Family care / family relocation |
| **C** | Internal displacement due to conflict |
| **D** | Migration abroad (not to liberated areas) |
| **E** | Partial / selective resistance participation |

### STEP 4 — UNKNOWN

- Exact number of actual movers  
- Destinations  
- Internal vs external ratio  
- Civilian survival vs armed participation ratio  
- Primary motives  
- Whether the trend is increasing over time  

### STEP 5 — FINAL CALIBRATION

```
🟡 MEDIUM
PLAUSIBLE HYPOTHESIS
NOT YET PROVEN
```

**Meaning:**  
Not “impossible.”  
Not “certain.”  
From current evidence: a plausible hypothesis that is not yet strong enough for large-scale prediction or proven reality.

---

## VI. Critical Distinctions

| Level | Meaning | Requirement |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| 🟢 **POTENTIALITY** | “It is possible” | Conditions create possibility |
| 🔵 **PREDICTION** | “It will happen” | Trend data + forecasting logic |
| 🔴 **PROVEN REALITY** | “It is happening / has happened” | Measurement + verification |

### Sentinel Rule

```
“People can move”     → hypothesis
“People are moving”   → requires measurement
“Why they move”       → requires causation
“What they will do”   → prediction
```

## VIII. Student 4 Reopened Test

1. Does the existence of a Push Factor prove people are actually leaving?  
2. If people are leaving, can we automatically conclude they are going to liberated areas?  
3. Can we say everyone who reaches a liberated area will join resistance?  
4. Can a recruitment figure of 100 represent an entire Gen Z generation movement?  
5. What additional evidence would raise confidence from 🟡 Medium to 🟢 High?

---

## IX. Sentinel Final Conclusion

Do not call what is “possible” what is “already happening.”  
Do not turn “it is happening” into “why it is happening” without causation evidence.  
Do not turn the presence of factors into a forecast of the future.

**Demand Evidence at every step:**

```
Potentiality → Measurement → Causation → Prediction
```

Do not skip these stages.

> **REALITY BEFORE NARRATIVE**

This is the TAI-3 Intelligence Calibration Discipline.
