3. 🇬🇧 English Translation & Analytical Synthesis
> "Doubt is the hindrance of Vicikiccha. It prevents the arising of Vipassana insight and blocks the attainment of Path and Fruition (Magga-Phala). Overcoming it through direct empirical practice is crucial... This Satipatthana is the Ekayano Magga—the sole way, continuous from beginning to middle to end."
> — Ven. Mahasi Sayadaw / SSISM Epistemic Synthesis
